## Decisions.csv review: a real pattern, not fixable here

Read the last ~130 rows of state/champion/decisions.csv and the tails of
challenger1-3. No contradictions between stated reason and action anywhere,
and the "none: no fill possible (no cash or no position)" rows are not
random — they trace to one consistent cause worth recording.

**champion's DOT has been unable to reach its target weight for two straight
days.** Sequence from decisions.csv: DOT entered at 1789921304 (2026-09-20
~15:00Z) with a `buy` that only reached weight 0.0 -> ~0.009 (target was
0.125 after the gross-cap scale-down), immediately followed by ~13 straight
hours of `none, no fill possible (no cash or no position)` while its target
sat at 0.10 and its held weight stayed frozen at 0.009. A second partial buy
at 1790054477 nudged it to 0.026. It has been stuck at 0.026 against a
target of 0.10-0.11 for the 16 hours since, right up to the last run
(2026-09-22 21:20Z). champion's AVAX shows the same symptom: `enter long`
firing with target 0.0957-0.238 and getting `no fill possible` repeatedly.
challenger3 (also long-only, similar sizing) shows the identical shape on
its own DOT and DOGE.

Root cause, from reading bot/paper.py and bot/run.py (both protected, so I
can't change this): `Account.trade()` only buys up to `cash / (1+fee)`, and
`bot/run.py` executes one pair's trade per iteration in the fixed pairs
order from configs/risk.yaml (BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, AVAX, LINK, XRP, DOGE, DOT,
LTC), spending shared cash as it goes. `risk.apply_limits` scales the whole
target vector down so gross exposure clears `max_gross_weight`, but that
scaling is done on the signal weights, not on actual available cash — it
doesn't know that seven other pairs are already sitting within their own
0.05 rebalance threshold and have no reason to trim. So once the book is
fully invested and diversified across pairs that are each individually near
their own target, a pair that enters late or partially (DOT here) or a pair
whose signal turns on after everyone else is settled (AVAX here) can be
priced out indefinitely: there's no cash, and nothing is selling to make
any, because nothing else's weight change crosses the rebalance threshold.
It isn't a random rounding blip — it's now run for 2+ days straight on DOT.

This means the champion and challenger3 accounts are currently running
meaningfully underweight DOT (and flat AVAX) relative to what ts_momentum
and swing_reversal actually want, which will understate whatever edge those
two pairs would have contributed, and the "by pair" bps-per-round-trip
figures in state/summary.md for DOT specifically are computed on a much
smaller traded notional than the strategy intended. I can't fix this: the
partial-fill cash logic (bot/paper.py), the gross-weight scaling
(bot/risk.py), and the execution loop's fixed iteration order (bot/run.py)
are all protected. Flagging for Fin per the hard rule: apply_limits scaling
signal weights to fit a gross cap without regard to how much of that gross
is actually realisable in cash, combined with a rebalance threshold that
lets already-settled pairs sit still instead of trimming to fund laggards,
looks like the wrong design once the book is this fully invested. A fairer
allocator would either size new/growing positions out of a reserved cash
slice, or process the trade loop in an order that isn't a static function of
pairs: [...] (e.g. largest shortfall first), or both.

No missing hours in the Data section for any pair. No bug in bot/strategy.py
found — this lives entirely in the protected execution path, so no fix goes
in this PR.
