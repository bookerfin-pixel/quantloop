## Finding: the backtest gate now replays a real ~50-80% crash

`state/history/*.csv` (the two year Coinbase backfill) was written in commit
0903d27, 2026-09-17T07:09Z — after H1 was tested and merged
(2026-09-16T04:36Z) and after yesterday's notes-only run. Today is the first
run with the full backfill in place, and the default backtest window (last
365 days) now actually has 365 days of history to use instead of the ~20
days available when H0 and H1 were written.

Checked what that year actually did, pair by pair (last 365 days of
state/history, peak to trough):

- BTC -46% (peak to trough -54%)
- ETH -57% (-69%)
- SOL -61% (-76%)
- ADA -81% (-86%)
- AVAX -74% (-84%)
- LINK -58% (-74%)
- XRP -66% (-69%)
- DOGE -69% (-77%)
- DOT -81% (-85%)
- LTC -62% (-71%)

Every pair in the universe is down 46-81% over the trailing year, with
drawdowns from peak of 54-86%. This is not the choppy-but-roughly-flat 21 day
window H0 and H1 were written against; it is a severe, sustained bear market
across the whole universe.

Ran `python -m bot.backtest --config <cfg>` (default 365 day window) against
both the champion and the running challenger to see how they hold up over the
full window, then tried three filtered variants to see if a smarter entry
rule could survive it. All backtests below use the standard cost model
(10 bps fee + 5 bps slippage per side), same as the gate.

|---|---|---|---|---|

None of these are in the ledger — I built and backtested them to see whether
any reasonable filter could keep a long only, spot only strategy out of this
drawdown, before deciding whether a slot was worth filling today. None came
close to `backtest_gate`'s bar (cost_coverage >= 1.0, max_drawdown >= -30%,
in configs/risk.yaml). The best of the three (vol_regime_momentum) still lost
43% with a 59% drawdown. I did not commit any of these; bot/strategy.py and
configs/challenger2.yaml are back to what they were this morning.

The mechanism problem is not subtle: with no shorting, a strategy that is
ever long during a year where every pair fell 46-81% needs to time entries
and exits almost perfectly to come out ahead, and a fixed EMA or vol filter
just thins out how often it tries, not how right it is when it does. Cutting
exposure earlier (vol_regime_momentum, avg gross exposure 0.40 vs H0's 0.53)
helped some but nowhere near enough.

## Why I did not fill a slot today

`gate/backtest_gate.py` (protected) always reruns the changed slot's backtest
over `backtest_gate.max_days` = 365 (protected, in configs/risk.yaml) — not
whatever window I paste into the ledger entry. Given the table above, I don't
have a hypothesis I can honestly write "Expected gross bps per round trip"
for that would also survive that actual gate check; every direction I tried
today fails it by a wide margin over the real replay window. Submitting one
anyway would just waste a PR cycle on a rejection I can already see coming
(and per the ledger format, the expected bps number has to reflect the
mechanism, not be picked to clear the 60 bps floor).

This is a systemic point, not a per-hypothesis one: since the two-year
backfill landed this morning, `backtest_gate`'s fixed 365-day replay window
now spans a specific, severe historical crash for every pair. That may make
it very hard for *any* new challenger to clear the plausibility gate until
the window rolls past this period, unless the hypothesis is one that trades
rarely enough, and precisely enough, to profit inside a downtrend — which is
a much narrower ask than what challenger1 or the champion were built to do.
I'm flagging this for Fin rather than trying to force something through:
worth deciding whether backtest_gate should use a shorter or rolling window,
or whether this is intended and the bar is just genuinely this high for now.
