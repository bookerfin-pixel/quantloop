- Champion, challenger1, challenger2, challenger4: reasons match actions, no

- challenger3 (H3): a bug in `swing_reversal` (bot/strategy.py:176). Earlier notes
  called the LTC/LINK re-entries "price hovering at the stop, the rule". On
  reading the code and decisions.csv I now think it is a design flaw, not just
  the rule working:
  - Entry is a level test, not a cross: `price > swing_high` stays true for as
    long as price sits above the reaction high, however long ago it broke out.
  - Exit is `price <= min(close[-49:-1])`, the lowest close of the last 48h.
    After a rally that then fades, the current close is routinely a fresh 48h
    low while still above the reaction high.
  - So the exit fires, and the next hour the entry condition is still true, and
    it buys again. LINK on 2026-10-02: entry 20:23Z at 13.59, exit 21:23Z on
    "closed 13.56 below the 48h low 13.59". Earlier LINK sequences in
    decisions.csv: four enter/exit pairs inside a few hours (13.69 -> 13.69,
    13.64 -> 13.78, ...), and 14.36 in, 14.14 out, 13.98 in, 13.63 out,
    13.62 in, 13.62 out. Each pair costs about 30 bps (more on LINK, half
    spread 2-4 bps) for no move captured.
  - Effect on the test: H3 has 123 fills in 7 days (0.18 per pair per day against
    the 0.05 the backtest gate saw: 192 trades a year), and realised bps per
    round trip will be diluted by these zero-move loops. The backtest figure of
    192 trades implies the loop is much rarer in the 365 day sample than in
    this volatile rally (LINK realised vol 80-100%).
  - Not fixed now: any fix (require a fresh cross of the reaction high, or a
    cooldown after a stop exit) changes H3's behaviour mid-window and would
    confound the running test. Revisit when challenger3 is free or at its
    verdict (~2026-11-24). Read the verdict knowing a share of H3's fills are
    this loop; report gross bps per round trip with and without round trips
    shorter than 3 hours.
